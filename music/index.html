Side A

Six players.
Six singers.
One very
flexible band.

The Jersey Ramble started the way most good bands start — a garage, a couple of amps that were too big for the room, and a standing argument about tempo. Six of us now. Fathers, sons, and friends who have been playing together long enough to argue like family.

Here's the useful part: everybody sings, and most of us play more than one thing. Guitars trade off. Keys and organ swap hands mid-set. That's why you can book two of us or all six and still get a band — not a smaller version of one.

We play classic rock like we mean it, because we do. Then we slip in an original or two while the floor is still full and nobody's looking. Most nights, people can't tell which is which. We take that as the compliment it is.

— The Ramble, somewhere in Jersey
